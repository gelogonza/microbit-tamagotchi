# Project Mission — Interactive Artifact

## Why this folder exists

This is a place to make one small interactive artifact and learn how it works.
The finished object matters, but the larger purpose is to build the habit of
connecting an intention to code, a test, an observation, and a revision.

## What a successful project shows

By the end of Unit 1, a reader should be able to see:

- **An interaction with a purpose.** A person can do something with the
  artifact and get a meaningful response.
- **A student-owned explanation.** You can describe the important choices,
  uncertainties, and next questions in your own words.
- **Evidence of learning.** The folder shows how an idea became code, what you
  tested, what happened, and what you changed afterward.

## The working cycle

```
knowledge → intention → code → test → observation → revision
             ↖ curriculum record ↗
```

Do not hide the uncertain parts. A useful project makes its questions visible
and answers them a small piece at a time.

## Who decides

You decide what to build and what the observations mean. An AI agent may help
you locate, explain, question, and propose small changes; you review, test, and
remain responsible for every submitted claim and line of code. Full working
rules are in `AGENTS.md`.
