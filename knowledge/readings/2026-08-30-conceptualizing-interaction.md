To me **interactivity** means allowing users to interact with something and that within itself has multiple levels of interactivity. For example videogames have load of interactivity due to the amount of buttons you press to perform certain actions. Or like playing a game on the Wii. You have to move your entire body for some of those games in order for your character to perform the same/similar actions within the game. 

- One example of interactions I have everyday is using my laptop to take notes or use photoshop. A person opens up the app, types their notes, and writes notes about important topics or subjects that they are learning about. Same thing with photoshop. A person will learn how to use, then apply changes to the file/design they are creating and use different features of the app to create new things. 
- Another example would be to use your phone to listen to music. A user would open their phone, open their music app, type in what song they want to listen to, play that song, they can skip, rewind, shuffle, or make a playlist. I personally interact with this everyday and listen to music everyday. 
- Or say you use my website https://motion-canvas-seven.vercel.app/instrument for this you interact with your body/hands to control the sound on the website. It looks at your position on the camera and the sounds change with the way you move; making the website interactive in not just the typical way of navigating through a website, but instead moving your body to manipulate sound. 

I want my micro:bit to also have some sort of interactivity like that as well, where a person can press buttons or shake it so it performs a specific action.
