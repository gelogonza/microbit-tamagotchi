# Goal log

Dated record of how the main goal changed. Append-only — never edit or delete an
earlier entry.

The current goal lives at the top of `../learning-manual.html` and gets
overwritten. This file is where the previous versions go, with the reason.

**Revisit every two weeks or every third pass, whichever comes first.** Ask the
folder: `revisit goal`.

Changing your goal is normal. You will discover the project is not what you
thought, or that the interesting problem is somewhere else, or that what you
wanted to learn was three things and only one of them mattered. Record the shift
and why — that reasoning is often the most substantial thing in the manual.

Leaving it unchanged is also fine, but say so and say why. A goal that never
changes across a semester is usually a goal nobody reread.

Format: newest first.

## [date] — Revisited, unchanged

Still: [restate it]. Reread after pass 03. Still what I want, though I now think
the hard part is [X] rather than [Y] as I originally assumed.
